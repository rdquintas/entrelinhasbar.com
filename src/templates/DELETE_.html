Learn More
  1. 2008

    The Beginning

    Founded with a vision to create a space where music, creativity, and community come together.

  2. 2012

    Expanding Horizons

    Attracting international artists and a global audience. This marked the start of our journey to becoming a truly world-renowned festival.

  3. 2016

    Sustainability Initiative

    Committed to reducing our environmental footprint, Poize introduced its sustainability program

  4. 2019

    Record-Breaking Attendance

    With over 100,000 attendees, Poize hit a major milestone, becoming one of the largest music festivals in the region.

  5. 2021

    Largest Festival Ever

    Poize shattered all expectations, hosting the largest festival in its history. With more than 150,000 attendees and a lineup of top-tier international artists, it solidified its place as one of the most iconic music festivals worldwide.

  6. 2024

    The Future of Poize

    As we look ahead, Poize continues to innovate, bringing even more immersive experiences and pushing the boundaries of live music, art, and community.
